# Competency scorecard (0–4)

Score from evidence, not confidence.

- **0 — Unknown:** cannot explain or perform the task.
- **1 — Guided:** can follow exact instructions.
- **2 — Assisted:** can execute with notes and explain common outputs.
- **3 — Independent:** solves a new but bounded scenario, verifies recovery and writes a clear report.
- **4 — Reliable under ambiguity:** tests competing hypotheses, avoids unsafe changes, considers second-order effects, automates recurrence prevention, communicates clearly and mentors another learner.
