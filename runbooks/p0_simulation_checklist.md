# P0 simulation scorecard

A P0 tabletop is about controlling impact and communication, not typing fastest.

Score each 0–2 (0 absent/unsafe, 1 partial, 2 clear and complete):
- User/business impact and scope quantified.
- Severity declared according to the stated policy.
- Incident commander / technical lead / communications roles assigned when possible.
- Timeline and evidence preserved.
- Facts separated from hypotheses.
- Multiple hypotheses and discriminating tests stated.
- Mitigation selected with risk, stop condition and rollback.
- Retry/overload/capacity second-order effects considered.
- Stakeholder update concise with next update time.
- Recovery validated against user-facing SLI.
- Follow-up action items have owners and measurable verification.

Maximum 22. Aim ≥18 on three different tabletop scenarios, with no zero in safety, communication or validation. The score is training feedback, not a real-world qualification.
